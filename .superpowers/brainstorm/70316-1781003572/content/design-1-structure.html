Key moves:
  • commands/ → removed entirely. Skills replace them — Claude discovers and invokes them via /skill-name or proactively.
  • 7 orchestrator agents → removed. Their content migrates into Skills.
  • IC + Coder → removed. Replaced by the subagent-driven-development Skill.
  • 6 worker agents → unchanged. Explore-type write-safety preserved.
  • hooks/ → added. session-start script injects skill awareness at every session open.
